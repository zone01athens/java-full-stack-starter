# Cohort schedule | Europe/Athens

Budget 15 hours per student per week. Keep the same team of three for the three raids where Platform assignment rules allow it. Publish named teams and dated slots before the piscine starts. Use the actual Athens event calendar to place exams and raids.

## Collect availability

Send each participant the same request seven days before kickoff. Keep individual responses in a staff-controlled sheet. Share only the final roster and agreed slots with participants.

> Please reply by [date and time] with your recurring availability for the piscine. For each day, give the exact start and end times you can commit, in Athens time, and whether you can attend campus or join online. Mark fixed commitments and date-specific exceptions. Confirm that you can reserve 15 hours a week and list any conflicts with the attached exam and raid windows. We will use your answers to assign a team of three and publish its schedule before kickoff.

Request availability rather than employer names or reasons for an absence. List multiple intervals separately. Use the [availability template](availability-template.csv). Provide a dated calendar with the request; do not promise a slot before the event dates are known.

## Form teams and freeze the calendar

1. Record the Platform opening and closing times for each raid and the exam times. Each raid currently has a configured duration of 52 hours. Use the actual event times in Athens.
2. Remove fixed commitments and exceptions from each student's available intervals. Apply campus opening hours to in-person slots (weekdays 09:00–24:00; weekends 12:00–20:00).
3. For every candidate trio, intersect all three calendars inside each raid window. Require two shared three-hour blocks per raid with a compatible location or online mode. Check that the exam fits each student's calendar and their weekly plan totals 15 hours.
4. Select disjoint trios covering the cohort. Prefer keeping teams intact across raids, then maximize common backup time. Check all trios together before reserving individuals. If the headcount or overlap leaves someone unmatched, ask for another slot or coordinate another event with staff. Never assume availability or silently assign a fourth member.
5. Three days before kickoff, send each student a team ID, two teammates, exact dates, times, location and backup slot. Obtain individual confirmation. Freeze the roster one day before kickoff. Staff resolve exceptions with affected teams before changing a booking.

## Weekly time budget

| Week type | Fixed shared or exam time | Other scheduled work | Total |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quest week | 2 × 3 h team practice | 3 × 3 h individual work and review | 15 h |
| Raid plus 4 h exam | 2 × 3 h raid + 4 h exam | 5 h quests, preparation and corrections | 15 h |
| Raid plus 8 h exam | 2 × 3 h raid + 8 h exam | 1 h preparation or corrections | 15 h |

The exam durations are configured windows. Reserve the full window unless staff confirm a shorter attendance requirement. For a quest-only week, one possible calendar is Mon/Wed/Fri 18:00–21:00 individual work and Tue/Thu 18:00–21:00 team practice. Replace it with each team's confirmed times. For a raid, place both shared blocks inside its event window; never carry the weekday example over automatically.

## Raid execution

First block: spend 15 minutes agreeing interfaces and acceptance tests. Split the remaining work into three independently testable tasks. Each member owns one task and reviews another member's change. Integrate a working slice before the block ends.

Second block: rotate integration responsibility, merge remaining changes, test from a clean checkout and rehearse the audit together. Reserve the final 30 minutes for the Gitea submission and Platform readback. Report blocked work immediately in the team channel.

After each week record completed Platform stages, unresolved blockers and actual hours. If the workload exceeds 15 hours, move optional practice or arrange another supported event. Completion depends on recorded results and scheduled assessments.

Publish one row per shared block in the [team calendar](team-calendar-template.csv). Use real dates, including the backup slot. Send individual work blocks directly to each participant. Publish only confirmed teams.
